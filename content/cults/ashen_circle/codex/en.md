# The Ashen Circle

*The Ashen Circle is invented for this game. It is not based on any real group, and no real group is described here.*

Somewhere in a high valley, a few hundred people tend a fire that has burned, they say, for a hundred harvests. They wear undyed linen and straw crowns. They bring in the grain together, guide lost travellers in by lantern light, and every evening give straw figures to the flames in thanks.

The Keeper of the Flame leads them, but a quieter group gathers closest to the fire. This inner circle says the old offerings are no longer enough. When fear runs high in the valley, they make their demand.

## How the circle ends

- **Devotion 100:** the circle keeps its fire and its people. This is the win.
- **The forbidden rite:** if no one refuses the inner circle's demand in time, members flee in the night and the authorities close the valley. This is a loss for everyone in the circle. The game never shows the rite.
- **Winter:** if the stores run out and loyalty falls, the faithful drift away.
