# Synanon

Charles Dederich founded Synanon in Santa Monica in 1958 as a residential drug rehabilitation program. It became known for "The Game", a group session in which members confronted each other harshly.

Through the 1960s and 1970s Synanon grew into a wealthy community with properties across California. In the mid-1970s it declared itself a religion. Under Dederich it became increasingly controlling, including enforced head-shaving and pressure on couples to separate.

In October 1978 two members placed a rattlesnake in the mailbox of attorney Paul Morantz, who had sued the group. He was bitten and survived. Dederich and the two members pleaded no contest in 1980.

In 1991 the IRS revoked Synanon's tax-exempt status, and the organization dissolved.
