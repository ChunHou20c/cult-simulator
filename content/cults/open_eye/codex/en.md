# The Society of the Open Eye

*The Society of the Open Eye is invented for this game. It is not based on any real group, and no real group is described here. Its eye-in-a-triangle sign is drawn for this game, after the old "all-seeing eye" of folklore and conspiracy stories.*

Behind the locked bronze doors of an old bank, past the vault, there is a hall of white marble. Above its altar hangs a great eye inside a golden triangle, ringed with light. The members wear pale robes and gold pins. They hand brass keys to people who might be useful, trade whispers from their archive to powerful clients, and each night stand before the Eye to confess everything they have seen.

The Keeper of Records leads them, but a quieter inner circle holds the archive's last key. They say that watching is no longer enough. When fear runs high in the society, they make their demand.

## How the society ends

- **Secrets 100:** the society keeps its hall and its people. This is the win.
- **The forbidden rite:** if no one refuses the inner circle's demand in time, members flee in the night and the police chain the bronze doors. This is a loss for everyone in the society. The game never shows the rite.
- **Exposed:** if scrutiny reaches 100, the membership ledger leaks and the society dissolves.
