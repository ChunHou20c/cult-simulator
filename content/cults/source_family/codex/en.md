# The Source Family

The Source Family was a spiritual commune in Los Angeles led by Jim Baker, who called himself Father Yod. Baker opened The Source, one of the first organic vegetarian restaurants in the United States, on Sunset Boulevard around 1969. Its profits supported a household of about 140 followers who lived together in the Hollywood Hills, wore white robes and followed Baker's teachings.

The family's band, Ya Ho Wha 13, recorded improvised psychedelic albums that later became collector's items.

In 1974 the group sold the restaurant and moved to Hawaii. In August 1975 Baker died after a hang-gliding accident on Oahu. Without him the group slowly dispersed.
