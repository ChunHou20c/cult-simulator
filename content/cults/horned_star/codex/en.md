# The Order of the Horned Star

*The Order of the Horned Star is invented for this game. It is not based on any real group, and no real group is described here. Its idol is drawn for this game, after the old horned-goat figure of folklore and occult books.*

Beneath an old city, down a stair behind a bookshop, a few hundred people meet by candlelight. They wear black robes and gold medallions. They read the stars for paying patrons, pass sealed invitations to curious guests, and kneel each night before the Horned One: a winged stone goat with a star on its brow and a flame between its horns.

The Magister leads them, but a quieter group keeps the keys to the lower rooms. This inner lodge says that kneeling is no longer enough. When fear runs high in the order, they make their demand.

## How the order ends

- **Illumination 100:** the order keeps its lodge and its people. This is the win.
- **The forbidden rite:** if no one refuses the inner lodge's demand in time, members flee in the night and the police seal the stair. This is a loss for everyone in the order. The game never shows the rite.
- **Unmasked:** if scrutiny reaches 100, the newspapers print every name and the order breaks apart.
