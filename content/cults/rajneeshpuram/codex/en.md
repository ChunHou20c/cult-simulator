# Rajneeshpuram

In 1981 followers of the Indian guru Bhagwan Shree Rajneesh bought the Big Muddy Ranch in Wasco County, Oregon, and began building a city. Rajneeshpuram was incorporated in 1982 and grew to several thousand residents, with its own police, airstrip and reservoir. Ma Anand Sheela, Rajneesh's secretary, ran the commune day to day.

Conflict with neighbours and the state grew over land use and the commune's attempt to take over local government. In September 1984 commune leaders contaminated salad bars in The Dalles with salmonella, sickening 751 people, in an attempt to sway a county election.

In 1985 Sheela fled to Europe. Rajneesh was arrested in North Carolina in October 1985, pleaded guilty to immigration fraud, and was deported. Sheela was later extradited and convicted. The city's incorporation was struck down and the commune emptied.
